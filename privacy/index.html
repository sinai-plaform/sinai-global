Privacy policy

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit sinai-global.com, including the Downline HQ pages. It is short because the site collects very little.

Who runs this site

sinai-global.com is operated by Liran Dandekar, Shlomi, Israel, under the name SINAI Global. Questions about privacy: hello@sinai-global.com.

What the site collects

Nothing that identifies you. The site has no forms, accounts or sign-in, and sets no advertising or tracking cookies. When you pick a language, the choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) so the site opens in that language next time. It is not sent to us.

Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. It works without cookies, does not track you across sites, and shows us only aggregated numbers such as page views, referring sites and countries.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and its domain is managed with Cloudflare. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. As with any website, these providers receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type in order to deliver the pages, under their own privacy policies.

When you contact us

If you write to us by email or WhatsApp, we use your name, contact details and message only to reply and to handle your request. We do not sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list. WhatsApp messages are also subject to WhatsApp's own terms.

Our apps

JaSale, SINAI Club, SINAI Cinema and Downline HQ each have their own privacy policy inside the app. That policy, not this one, applies to the data you enter when you use them.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at hello@sinai-global.com. You can clear the saved language at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
