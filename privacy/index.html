Privacy policy

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit sinai-global.com, including the Downline HQ pages, and when you talk to the chat on the site.

Who runs the site

The site is operated by Liran Dandekar, Shlomi, Israel, under the name SINAI Global. Privacy questions: hello@sinai-global.com.

What is stored in your browser

The language and currency you chose, and the country you connected from (so prices show in the right currency). All of these are stored only in your browser (local storage) and are not sent to us. The site uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

The chat on the site

When you write in the chat, we keep the conversation and whatever you chose to tell us: your name, phone or e-mail, details about your business and what it needs.

  • We use this information to answer you, prepare an offer and get back to you.
  • When you leave your details, a summary of your request is e-mailed to a representative of our sales team.
  • Chat replies are written with the help of an AI model from an outside provider (Google). The provider processes the content of the conversation to write a reply.
  • To prevent abuse, we keep a one-way encrypted version of your IP address, not the address itself.
  • We won't sell the information, pass it to others for marketing, or add you to a mailing list.
  • Please don't share sensitive information or internal business data in the chat. It isn't needed at this stage.

How long we keep it

Conversations and requests are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted, unless you become a customer.

Providers

  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare.
  • The chat and requests are stored in a Supabase database.
  • E-mails are sent through Resend.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

As with any website, these providers receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies.

Statistics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies and shows us only aggregate data.

When you e-mail us

We use your name, contact details and message only to answer and handle your request.

Our apps

JaSale, SINAI Club, SINAI Cinema and Downline HQ have their own privacy policies, which apply to the information you enter in them.

Your choices

You can ask to know what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, at hello@sinai-global.com. You can delete what is stored in your browser at any time by clearing the site's data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page.
