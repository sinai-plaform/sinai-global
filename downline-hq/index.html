Downline HQ · In development

Your whole network on one screen.

Downline HQ gives independent distributors a personal landing page, a CRM for their leads, and a live tree of everyone under them. Leaders see their full downline. The network owner decides who sees and edits what.

Join the early list

What's inside

Built for direct-sales teams

A personal landing page

Every distributor gets their own page with their photo, their story and their contact button. Prices and products stay set by the network.

Lead CRM

Every contact from the page lands as a lead with tasks and follow-ups, so nobody falls through the cracks.

Live network tree

Leaders see everyone below them at any depth, with each person's activity in one place.

Permissions you control

Distributors edit only their own profile. Only an authorized leader can open a new distributor. The owner sets the rules.

Raw media library

Product photos, box shots and certificates to build your own posts, instead of the same copy-paste post everyone shares.

Any company, any product

Downline HQ is not tied to one brand. A network of any company can run on it with its own products.

How it works

Set up the network

The owner adds the products and decides what each role can see and do.

Open distributors

Leaders open accounts for their team. Each one gets a page and a login.

Share the page

Distributors share their personal link. Every inquiry becomes a lead.

Follow the tree

Leaders track leads and activity across their whole downline.

Questions

Is Downline HQ available now?

It is in development and used inside one network today. We are opening it to more networks gradually. Join the early list and we will contact you.

Does it work only for one company's products?

No. It is built so any direct-sales company's distributors can use it with their own products.

Can a distributor change prices or products?

No. Distributors edit their own profile, photo and personal text. Prices, products and the people above them are set by the network.

Who sees my team's data?

You see everyone below you. The people above you see you. The network owner controls all permissions.
