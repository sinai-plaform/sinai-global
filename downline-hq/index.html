Downline HQ · In development

Your whole network on one screen.

Downline HQ gives independent distributors a personal landing page, a smart lead CRM, one-click sharing that tracks itself, and an AI studio that makes posts and videos from your products. Team Leaders see their whole team. The Network Leader decides who sees and edits what.

What's inside

Built for direct-sales teams

One-click sharing, full tracking

Share any post, video or page to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok with a personal short link. Opens, people, leads and orders are counted by themselves and flow up the tree — nobody reports anything.

AI content studio

Pick a product, a goal and a platform, add your own story and style — get a ready post, image, reel or banner. Every result is different, so the platforms never punish duplicate content.

The next action, every morning

3–5 tasks a day, each with its reason: a lead waiting three days, a customer due to reorder, a new team member who went quiet. One tap opens WhatsApp with a ready message.

Rewards and ranks

Your company's compensation plan as data: active and qualified status, left and right volume, exactly what's missing for the next rank, and an income estimate. Upload the plan document — the AI reads it and you approve.

Compliance before posting

Every post and video is checked for medical claims, income promises and brand misuse, with a compliant rewrite suggested. The Network Leader can add a manual approval step.

Smart AI insights

A short daily note for leaders in plain words: whose team slowed down, which content works three times better, who is close to the next rank — with the numbers behind it.

A personal landing page

Every distributor gets their own page with their photo, their story and their contact button. Prices, ingredients and claims stay locked to the approved product library.

Lead CRM and action hour

Every inquiry lands as a lead with follow-ups. A daily action hour with a goal and message templates turns intentions into conversations.

Live network tree

Team Leaders see everyone below them at any depth, with each person's activity in one place.

Permissions you control

Distributors edit only their own profile. Only an authorized Team Leader can open a new distributor. The Network Leader sets the rules.

Tokens, packs and quotas

AI creations run on tokens with a free monthly quota per plan. The Network Leader sets prices and packs, and can give tokens to the team.

Any company, any product

Downline HQ is not tied to one brand. A network of any company runs on it with its own products and its own compensation plan.

How it works

Set up the network

The Network Leader adds the products and the compensation plan, and decides what each role can see and do.

Open distributors

Team Leaders open accounts for their team. Each one gets a page, a studio and a login.

Share and create

Distributors share from the library or the studio in one click. Every open is counted, every inquiry becomes a lead.

Lead with data

Leaders see what works, who is close to a rank and who needs a call — every morning.

FAQ

Is Downline HQ available now?

It is in development and used inside one network today. We are opening it to more networks gradually. Join the early list and we will contact you.

Does it work only for one company's products?

No. Any direct-sales company's distributors can use it with their own products and their own compensation plan.

Can a distributor change prices or products?

No. Distributors edit their own profile, photo and personal text. Prices, products, claims and the people above them are set by the network.

Who sees my team's data?

You see everyone below you. The people above you see you. The Network Leader controls all permissions.

Is sharing tracked with cookies?

No. Opens are counted on our server from the personal short link. No third-party tracking cookies, no pixels.
