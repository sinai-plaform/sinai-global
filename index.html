JaSale

The field-sales app for companies that sell through agents

Built first for a field agent in the pharma sector who worked from PDF catalogs and paper notes. JaSale plans the agent's week, shows the manager the whole team at a glance, and keeps working on the road with no signal.

  • A smart weekly plan built from open debts, the days since each customer's last visit and order, and the visit rhythm their grade calls for. Anyone seen in the last 14 days stays off the list.
  • Each day covers one area, with stops lined up in driving order and opened in Waze or Google Maps
  • Orders with product photos, price lists and step-based bonuses in one tap, and payments matched to each open invoice, even with no signal
  • For the manager, one board: every agent's visits, orders and collections today, missed visits, debts past 90 days, and weekly plans to approve or send back with a note
  • Targets and commission tiers with a month-end forecast at the current pace, sales competitions with a leaderboard, real-time stock and a dispatch board for trucks and couriers

SG-02Live

SINAI Club

Training and player development for youth football, built on the pitch

Built from more than a decade of coaching youth football, with teams from kindergarten to 10th grade. The coach plans the season ahead, notes what each player does during practice, and the notes build a player card over time. Parents and players get their own view, and the coach side works with no internet.

  • A smart training plan: choose a theme and a period, and every session is built for the team's age group, moving week by week from teaching to practice to a test game
  • Every session has a coach's structure: warm-up, rondo, main block and a closing game, drawn from 66 drills inspired by La Masia, with animated demos and an injury-prevention warm-up each week
  • Hold the mic and speak during practice; the note turns into ratings for the right players and attributes
  • A Football Manager-style 1–20 player card, built from many small observations, with recent sessions weighted more
  • End-of-session game: teams split automatically by level or by hand, with notes during the game

SG-03In development

Downline HQ

CRM, network tree and personal sales pages for direct-sales teams

Built inside our own direct-sales network and designed for distributors of any company. Every distributor gets a personal sales page and a CRM for leads, orders and follow-up tasks. Leaders see their whole downline, and the network owner decides what each person may change. The admin runs in 9 languages.

  • A personal sales page for each distributor, with their photo, contact and payment methods, and a product chat that answers in their name
  • Lead CRM: import from CSV, Excel or a Google Sheets link, duplicate check, statuses, reminders and orders
  • Live network tree: leaders see everyone below them at any depth; prices, products and permissions stay with the owner
  • Click tracking per channel and account, and a shared library of raw product media

How we build

Same four steps every time

Ride along

Spend a day with the user. Map what they do on paper, in PDFs and in their head.

Model the data

Turn the workflow into tables, roles and permissions before any screen exists.

Ship to the phone

A first working version in the user's hands within days, not a slide deck.

Iterate weekly

Each release starts from what we heard in the field, so the product keeps pace with the work.
