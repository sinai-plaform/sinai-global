JaSale

The field-sales app for distributors and their agents

Built first for a medical-supplies distributor whose agents worked from PDF catalogs and paper notes. JaSale puts the catalog, orders, routes and collections on the agent's phone, and fits any company that sells through agents in the field.

  • Order builder with VAT, bonus tiers and commission tracking
  • Weekly plan and daily driving routes by sales area, with Waze and Google Maps
  • Debt collection, live stock, warehouse picking and sales competitions

How we build

Same four steps every time

Ride along

Spend a day with the user. Map what they do on paper, in PDFs and in their head.

Model the data

Turn the workflow into tables, roles and permissions before any screen exists.

Ship to the phone

A first working version in the user's hands within days, not a slide deck.

Iterate weekly

Every release comes from field feedback. JaSale is past its 80th version.
